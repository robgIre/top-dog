# Top Dog 🐾 — Rocco's Training

A fun daily dog-training app so James can train Rocco (a 15-month-old Cavapoo) a little every
day, keep a streak, and earn rewards.

**Live:** https://robgire.github.io/top-dog/

## What it does
- **Daily plan** — 5 short drills a day, always including the two priorities: **Come Back (recall)** and **Drop It**.
- **Leveled journey** — 5 levels from *Puppy Basics* → *Good Manners* → *Recall Hero* → *Fetch & Drop* → *Trick Star*, with real positive-reinforcement coaching tips for every skill.
- **Mastery tracking** — rate how Rocco did (🌟/👍/🤔); each skill fills toward "Mastered" and Rocco earns an overall rank (Puppy → Good Boy → Clever Dog → Top Dog → Superstar).
- **Rewards** — a **5-day streak = €5**, a **10-day streak = €10**, then the streak resets and the cycle starts again. Miss a day and the streak resets to zero.
- **Tell Dad** — a one-tap share button (uses the phone's Share sheet / a pre-filled text) so James can send his streak to a parent when he hits a reward.
- Installable (PWA), works offline, auto-updates.

## Training strategy (built into the "Coach's tips")
Short sessions (3–5 min, 2–3×/day) · reward-based only, never punish · high-value treats for the
hard skills · one cue word per behaviour · build up Distance/Duration/Distraction one at a time ·
recall is always a party and practised on a long line first · always end on a win.

Everything is stored locally on the device. Not veterinary or professional behaviour advice.
